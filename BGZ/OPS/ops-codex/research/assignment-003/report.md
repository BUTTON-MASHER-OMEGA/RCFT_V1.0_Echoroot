## A. Mathematical model

The standard background is John Baez's [The Octonions, §2.4, Spinors and Trialities](https://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/octonions/node7.html). Spin(8) has distinct real eight-dimensional vector and half-spin modules. Their normed trilinear pairing has Spin(8) symmetry; outer automorphisms form S3 and permute the three representation types. Recovering a single unital octonion algebra from the three slots requires additional choices of unit vectors. These statements do not canonically identify the modules or assign them substrate meanings.

**Explicit model, verified below:** use real octonions O=H⊕H with Cayley–Dickson product

    (a,b)(c,d) = (ac − conjugate(d)b, da + b conjugate(c)).

Let V, S and C be three **typed mathematical copies** of O, carrying 8_v, 8_s and 8_c respectively. Set

    <x,y> = Re(conjugate(x)y),   T(v,s,c) = <v s,c>.

The Euclidean norms are representation-space forms, not physical distance. Multiplication defines L_v:S→C. It obeys L_vᵀL_v=||v||²I; hence T is normed and nondegenerate in the triality sense. It is not a group-invariant bilinear multiplication on one vector representation.

For an orthonormal basis let L_i=L_(e_i). The exact polarized identities are

    L_iᵀ L_j + L_jᵀ L_i = 2 δ_ij I.

Thus Γ(v)=[[0,L_vᵀ],[L_v,0]] is a real Clifford action with Γ(v)²=||v||²I. Its even action separates S and C. For F_ij=E_ij−E_ji, i<j, define

    A_v = F_ij,
    A_s = (L_iᵀ L_j)/2,
    A_c = (L_i L_jᵀ)/2.

**Verified:** all 28 generator triples are skew, and all 224 basis covariance identities satisfy

    A_c L_k − L_k A_s = L_(A_v e_k).

This is precisely infinitesimal invariance of T under the three *different* actions. Clifford commutators give Lie closure; four explicit generator-pair brackets were also checked. Since Spin(8) is connected, these invariant tensors are preserved by the integrated action. In the F_01 example, A_s²=A_c²=−I/4, so a 2π vector rotation acts as −I on the spinors. One cannot substitute an ordinary SO(8) action for these half-spin actions.

The three copies are formal representation slots only. The attempted OPS state embedding below uses one carrier, rather than introducing 24 substrate states, three physical spaces, or new fields.

## C. Invariant checks

### Pairing: valid lift, failed identical-slot action

**Verified:** P²=I, but L_1²=−I, although |L_1|=P. Thus unsigned multiplication support realizes the p matching; multiplication itself is not the frozen involution. P is not an octonion automorphism in this frame: it moves the unit. Likewise

    T(e0,e0,e0)=1,   T(Pe0,Pe0,Pe0)=0.

The tempting identification of p with the same operation in all three slots fails a necessary invariant, and that branch stops.

A genuine Spin lift nevertheless exists and is explicit. Let n_ab=(e_a−e_b)/sqrt(2). P is the product of the four hyperplane reflections for (01),(23),(45),(67). The even Clifford product Γ(n01)Γ(n23)Γ(n45)Γ(n67) has blocks P_s,P_c satisfying

    P_c L_k P_sᵀ = L_(P e_k).

All eight identities and both spinor norms/involutions passed. The matrices are stored in results.json; in this frame P_s is a signed p permutation and

    P_c = diag(−1,1,−1,1,1,−1,−1,1).

This is a mathematically meaningful triality-compatible realization of p in **one** slot, with appropriate companion actions in the other slots. It is available for arbitrary SO(8) rotations through the spin cover and therefore is not an OPS-specific explanation. The simultaneous sign ambiguity of the spin lift remains.

If one additionally assigns the same frozen Ω,A,B to all three slots, the companions do not preserve that assignment:

| P on vector slot | yes | yes | yes |

### Adjacency: matching agreement does not recover C8

**Derived and checked on one representative:** two distinct imaginary basis-unit supports act as XOR translations on F2³. The explicit |L_1|+|L_2| test has component sizes 4+4, while C8 has one component of size 8. Hence this natural two-matching construction fails; no alternative unit-pair search is needed.

## D. Nonassociativity and LCO compatibility

**Verified in OPS coordinates:**

    (e1⋆e2)⋆e7 = −e4,
    e1⋆(e2⋆e7) = +e4.

The associator is −2e4. A repeated-unit alternativity check passes, but that does not remove the displayed obstruction. Also e1⋆e1=−e0, so the eight positive basis states are not closed under direct multiplication. The direct eight-state multiplication branch stops. Quotienting basis units by sign recovers XOR composition but loses the signs responsible for this associator and does not by itself retain the oriented Ω data.

**Verified distinction:** L_1 L_2 ≠ L_(e1⋆e2); their difference has rank 4 and acts nontrivially on e7. Thus replacing operator composition by multiplication of octonionic labels changes the operation. By contrast (L_1L_2)L_7=L_1(L_2L_7), as ordinary matrix composition is associative. The Clifford/Spin construction uses this associative operator algebra, not reassociation of octonion products. Nonassociativity is therefore an obstruction to the tested direct-product substitution, **not an unavoidable obstruction to linear representations or Spin actions**.
