## Classification rules

Authority answers who can impose a requirement; evidence answers what supports an assertion. A locally authoritative invariant can still be an adopted design constraint without a necessity proof. Conversely, a proved finite theorem need not become a framework invariant. RCP-1.2 explicitly separates authority, dependencies and status, and limits body prose by the header and upstream contracts.
