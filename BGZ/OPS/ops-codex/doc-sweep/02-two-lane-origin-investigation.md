## Preservation vocabulary that needs a decision

For a realization claiming a symplectic action, use a declared vector space over a declared field, a nondegenerate **alternating** bilinear form Ω, and the condition SᵀΩS=Ω for transformations claimed symplectic. The current phrase “non-degenerate bilinear form” alone omits alternation. This clarifies the named structure; it does not show that each stochastic update kernel is a symplectic transformation. SIM16's stochastic obstructions warn against conflating those requirements. [rcmt/local/constraints/mode_space.md:51](C:/Users/blaur/projects/theory/rcmt/local/constraints/mode_space.md:51), [projects/Operational_Phase_Space/SIM16.2.py:224](C:/Users/blaur/projects/theory/projects/Operational_Phase_Space/SIM16.2.py:224)

“Representation independence” also needs a precise interpretation. Non-invertibility of Π records information loss in one direction; it is not a definition or proof of mutual irreducibility. `Π:Sp(2n)→G` currently names a group as source while discussing projection of mode configurations. The owner must declare whether it acts on states, representations, group elements or an observable algebra before making preservation and loss claims executable. ## Alternative formulations for human choice

**A — retain current RCMT requirement; annotate its status (least change).** “RCMT currently adopts a distinct symplectic mode-space structure and Lorentz-compatible geometric representation as model-local architectural requirements. Their necessity is not derived here from the RCF kernel or projection alone. The listed product groups are provisional implementations.” This preserves the present commitment and exposes the proof gap.

**B — require distinct functional roles, permit shared representation.** “RCMT requires separately specified mode-preservation and geometric-observation roles. A realization may share a carrier if it states the preserved structures, projection and information loss explicitly. Independent symmetry factors are not presumed.” This **changes** the present prohibition on a single representational domain and requires explicit architectural approval and propagation.

**C — make the current symplectic/geometric split an opt-in RCMT implementation profile.** “The dual-lane profile adopts an unbroken symplectic structure and Lorentz-compatible geometric representation; other RCMT profiles must state their own preservation and observation contracts.” This **changes** RCMT invariant 2 and related invariants/projection rules. Approval must identify the remaining RCMT-wide requirements and naming/version impact.

No option is selected or applied. A claim that two lanes are a theorem would additionally require a stated theorem, hypotheses, and proof; absent that artifact, do not upgrade A to mathematical necessity. No option authorizes a historical symmetry cascade to return as active architecture.

## Historical preservation rationale is not a surviving Time derivation

The initial Time text is a second relevant origin source, not an active license. It asserts that unitarity preserves symplectic memory and uses `[H,dρ(X)]=0` for `X` in a stabilizer of `h`, but does not supply the representation, form, carrier or a proof connecting these symbols to a necessary two-lane architecture. The current Time file expressly says those physical/symplectic readings belong downstream and requires an adopter to declare the optional generator compatibility data. This history explains why memory-preservation language appeared in the program; it cannot be cited as a current upstream derivation.
