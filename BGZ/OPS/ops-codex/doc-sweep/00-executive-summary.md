## Two-lane conclusion

The earliest RCF revision available locally (2026-07-23) asserted memory/containment and motion/direction as distinct functions, while allowing shared representation and accepting symplectic structure **or an equivalent informational invariant**. The 2026-07-25 revision explicitly made this profile optional. RCMT's first source commit already requires symplectic and Lorentz-compatible structures; the local record does not recover the reasoning that makes this stronger specialization necessary. Optional in RCF and required in RCMT is a legitimate architectural specialization, not itself a contradiction. The unsupported step would be calling the RCMT specialization a theorem of RCF or projection.
