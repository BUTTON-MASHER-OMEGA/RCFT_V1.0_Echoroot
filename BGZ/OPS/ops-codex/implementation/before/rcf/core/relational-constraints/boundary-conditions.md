### Core Definition

> **A boundary is the locus where relational constraints change discontinuously.**

Boundaries are not objects.
They are constraint transitions.

---

### Properties

* Boundaries define admissible interactions
* Reconfiguration is observed to occur preferentially at boundaries in current evaluations
* Cascades are observed to initiate where constraint gradients are steepest

A boundary may be:

* physical
* organizational
* cognitive
* informational

RCF does not distinguish at the core level.

### Summary

> Structure changes at its edges.
