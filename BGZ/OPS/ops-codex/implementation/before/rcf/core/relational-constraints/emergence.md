## Statement of the Constraint

**Constraint:**

> Structure emerges through the elimination of unstable relational modes, not through additive construction.

RCF does not build structure.
RCF filters structure.

Emergence is what remains.

---

## What Emergence Is (in RCF)

In RCF, emergence denotes:

> The persistence of relational structure after incompatible modes have been eliminated under constraint.

Emergence is **retentive**, not creative.
It is **selective**, not generative.
It is **residual**, not accumulative.

Nothing new is added.
What cannot survive is removed.

---

## What Emergence Is Not

Emergence is **not**:

- spontaneous creation
- bottom-up assembly
- magical complexity gain
- novelty injection
- hidden dynamics
- privileged scale transition

Any model that requires *adding* structure to explain emergence violates RCF.

---

## Emergence and Persistence

Persistence is the gate.

- Relations that fail under constraint disappear
- Relations that persist define structure
- Repeated persistence yields stability

Emergence is persistence observed across constraint gradients.

No persistence → no emergence.

---

## Emergence and Dominance

Dominant attractors shape emergence by:

- biasing which modes survive
- defining admissible alignments
- enforcing asymmetric constraint resolution

Emergent structure reflects **dominant relational geometry**, not intrinsic essence.

Change the dominant attractor, and different structure emerges.

---

## Emergence and ε

ε is required for emergence.

- Without ε, all resolution is immediate
- Without ε, no alternatives persist
- Without ε, no filtering can occur

Emergence occurs when:

1. Multiple admissible modes exist (ε > 0)
2. Constraints eliminate incompatible modes
3. A subset persists across interactions

Emergence is ε filtered by persistence.

---

## Cascades as Emergent Transitions

Emergence often appears discontinuous because:

- elimination occurs nonlinearly
- dominant modes shift
- ε collapses locally

These cascades are not phase transitions imposed by law.
They are **constraint saturation events**.

After cascade:
- the system has fewer admissible modes
- the surviving structure appears “new”
- nothing was added

---

## Scale Independence

Emergence in RCF is scale-agnostic.

The same eliminative pattern is used in the present formalism for:

- particles
- organisms
- cognition
- institutions
- cultures
- theories (including RCF itself)

Only constraint density and relational complexity change.

---

## Failure Modes

A model violates RCF if it:

- treats emergence as construction
- invokes hidden generators
- assumes scale-specific magic
- requires privileged micro-states
- depends on unexplained novelty sources

Such models explain emergence by renaming it.

---

## Summary

Emergence is not creation.
Emergence is survival.

What persists under constraint appears as structure.
What fails is forgotten.

RCF does not ask *where structure comes from*.
It asks *why some relations survive and others do not*.

That remainder is emergence.
