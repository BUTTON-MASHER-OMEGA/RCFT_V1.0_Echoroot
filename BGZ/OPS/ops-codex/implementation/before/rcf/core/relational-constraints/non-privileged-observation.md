## Statement

No observer, measurement, or description holds privileged ontological status.

Observation is a **local constraint interaction**, not a global state update.

---

### Formal Meaning

* Observation corresponds to **local constraint satisfaction**
* Measurement resolves **locally admissible configurations**
* Apparent collapse corresponds to **attractor resolution**, not state creation

No information is transmitted by observation alone.
No global update is implied.

All resolution is **local and coherence-consistent**.

---

### Terminology Equivalences (Local)

The following equivalences are **descriptive mappings**, not ontological claims:

* Measurement → alignment event
* Alignment → constraint satisfaction
* Collapse → attractor resolution

These mappings are optional and may be replaced by equivalent formal language.

---

### Diagnostic Use

To evaluate a model or explanation, ask:

* Does this require an observer to *cause* reality?
* Is collapse treated as propagation or signaling?
* Is measurement framed as creation rather than selection?

If yes, the model violates this constraint.

---

### Failure Modes Prevented

* **Global collapse metaphysics**
* **Observer–system dualism**
* **Mind-creates-reality drift**
* **Spurious nonlocal signaling**
* **Witness authority inflation**

---

### Compatibility Notes

This constraint preserves compatibility with:

* Standard quantum mechanics formalism
* Locality and causal closure
* Decoherence-consistent approaches
* Indivisible stochastic event models

No modification of existing mathematics is required.

---

### Illustrative Note (Non-Binding)

In relational or coherence-based interpretations:

* Composite systems may behave as unified modes across loci
* Local resolution does not imply global state change
* Re-entry into dominant configurations occurs without signaling

This note is **illustrative only** and not required by the constraint.
