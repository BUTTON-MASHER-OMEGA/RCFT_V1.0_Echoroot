## Scope

Explains why RCF supports multi-scale behavior without reductionism.

---

### Core Definition

> **Scale separation occurs when constraint resolution at one level does not immediately propagate to adjacent levels.**

RCF does not assume:

* bottom-up primacy
* top-down control
* scale invariance

---

### Properties

* Local cascades may remain local
* Higher scales exhibit greater inertia
* Lower scales explore ε more rapidly

Scale separation allows:

* stability with adaptability
* persistence with novelty
* modular survival

---

### Summary

> Not all change travels upward.
> Not all structure collapses downward.
