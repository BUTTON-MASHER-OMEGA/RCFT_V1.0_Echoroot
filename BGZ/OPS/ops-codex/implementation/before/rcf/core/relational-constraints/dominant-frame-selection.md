### Core Definition

> **A dominant frame is the system whose constraints most strongly limit admissible reconfiguration during interaction.**

Dominance is contextual.
It is not intrinsic.
It may shift over time.

---

### Properties

* Dominance emerges from relative constraint density
* Dominant systems impose alignment
* Subordinate systems resolve ε first

This applies to:

* physical interactions
* organizations
* cognition
* coupled models
