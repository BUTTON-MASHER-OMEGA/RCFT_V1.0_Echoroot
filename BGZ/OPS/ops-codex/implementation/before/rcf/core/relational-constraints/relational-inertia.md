## Scope

Defines why some systems resist reconfiguration more than others, without invoking mass, force, or dynamics.

---

### Core Definition

> **Relational inertia is a system’s resistance to reconfiguration due to accumulated constraint and memory mass.**

It is not motion.
It is not energy.
It is not momentum.

It is structural resistance.

---

### Properties

* Increases with constraint accumulation
* Increases with memory mass
* Narrows the ε-band
* Slows effective reconfiguration rate

Relational inertia explains why:

* large systems change slowly
* deeply structured systems resist perturbation
* stability scales with history

---

### Summary

> Systems do not persist because they move.
> They persist because they resist change.
