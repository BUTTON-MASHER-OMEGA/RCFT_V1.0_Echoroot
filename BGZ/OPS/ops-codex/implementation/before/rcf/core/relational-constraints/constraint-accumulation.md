---

##### Definition

> A cascade occurs when accumulated relational constraints eliminate all admissible continuations within a dominant attractor, forcing rapid reconfiguration toward a new persistent structure.

---

##### Constraint Accumulation

Constraint accumulation is the progressive reduction of admissible relational configurations through elimination, without immediate reorganization.

Accumulation alone does not imply change.

---

##### Cascade Threshold

A cascade is initiated when:

* no admissible continuation exists within the current attractor
* ε-band narrowing can no longer resolve conflicts
* persistence fails under existing constraints

---

##### Nature of Cascades

Cascades are:

* discrete
* local
* constraint-driven
* spectrally reconfigurative
* irreversible with respect to eliminated modes

They are not governed by time-continuous dynamics.

---

##### Role of ε

ε permits temporary evaluation of alternatives.
It does not prevent cascade when admissibility fails.

---

##### Spectral Interpretation

Cascades are observed to correspond to shifts in dominant spectral clustering under degeneracy pressure in current evaluations.

States change continuously.
Spectral organization changes discontinuously.

---

##### Summary

> Systems do not change because they are pushed.
> They change because they can no longer stay.
