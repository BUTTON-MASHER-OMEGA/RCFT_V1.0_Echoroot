## 1. Purpose

Define a representational ordering relation induced by constraint application.

The coherence gradient is descriptive, not dynamical.

---

## 2. Coherence Measure

Coherence C(R) may be any monotonic function of:

- spectral spacing
- loop tension
- degeneracy pressure
- relational overlap

No specific form is required.

---

## 3. Gradient Definition

Given R' = C(R):

    ∇_C R = C(R') − C(R)

A negative value indicates ordering toward higher coherence under C.

---

## 4. Attractor Basins

A relational attractor R* satisfies:

    ∇_C R* = 0

Nearby configurations reduce toward R* under constraint.

---

## 5. ε-Band

Residual admissible modes form an ε-band:

    R_eff = φ_1 + ε

ε defines local adaptability.

---

## 6. Reconfiguration Index

The reconfiguration index increments when:

    ∇_C R ≠ 0

This index is representational and does not imply temporal dynamics.

---

## 7. Eliminability

The coherence gradient is optional and may be removed without affecting RCD.
