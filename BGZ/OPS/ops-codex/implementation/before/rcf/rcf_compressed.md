## RCF Identity

- RCF is a constraint-based framework for analyzing persistence under relational pressure.
- RCF asks what survives declared constraints, not what fundamentally exists.
- RCF is a filter and lens, not a theory of everything, religion, worldview, ideology, diagnosis, moral framework, or metaphysical doctrine.
- RCF is:
  - eliminative rather than rescue-driven;
  - constraint-first rather than dynamics-first;
  - relational rather than object-primitive;
  - spectral rather than configuration-privileged;
  - modular rather than monolithic.
- Only the invariant kernel defines RCF.
- Formal languages, substrates, execution modules, geometries, metrics, and interpretations are subordinate and eliminable.
- RCF is self-applicable and receives no exemption from its own constraints.

## Invariant Kernel

The kernel contains exactly ten source invariants:

1. `I1 Persistence Under Constraint`: structures that persist under increasing relational constraint are meaningful; those that do not are eliminated.
2. `I2 Emergence via Elimination`: structure emerges through the elimination of unstable relations, not through additive construction.
3. `I3 Relational Primacy`: no entity possesses intrinsic meaning outside its relations.
4. `I4 Spectral Dominance`: persistent structure is encoded spectrally, not configurationally.
5. `I5 Constraint Over Dynamics`: RCF specifies admissibility conditions, not equations of motion.
6. `I6 Minimal Free Parameters`: valid RCF-compatible structures minimize tunable constants.
7. `I7 Non-Privileged Observers`: observation does not create reality; it constrains relational resolution.
8. `I8 Self-Applicability`: the framework must be applicable to itself without contradiction.
9. `I9 Epsilon-Allowance`: no complex system collapses to a single mode without residual alternatives.
10. `I10 Invariant Closure`: any extension or module must preserve all invariants, introduce no contradiction, and remain eliminable without damaging the core.

No module-local invariant, profile condition, validation gate, metric requirement, lane count, group structure, geometry, or implementation claim is an eleventh RCF invariant.
