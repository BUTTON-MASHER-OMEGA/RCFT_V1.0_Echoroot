### *Multiscale Aggregation of Local Coherence Dynamics*

The GCO module defines **global coherence** as the emergent, multiscale summary of recursively applied Local Coherence Operator (LCO) dynamics across nested systems.

GCO is **not** an independent execution rule.
It does not introduce a new operator that acts directly on states.

Instead:

> **GCO is the effective multiscale expression of recursively applied LCO dynamics across systems and subsystems.**

It aggregates:
- long‑horizon local coherence behavior,
- cross‑scale relational consistency,
- persistence of low‑cost relational patterns.

GCO produces **structural summaries**, not control actions.

---

## 1. Architectural Position

Execution flow:

- Time Core → Temporal Interface → LCO (local execution)
- Recursive application across systems / subsystems
- **GCO** (multiscale aggregation and persistence evaluation)
- Policy / downstream modules (interpretation, control, geometry)

GCO is therefore:

- **downstream** of recursive LCO behavior,
- **upstream** of any policy, control, or geometric interpretation,
- **geometry‑neutral**,
- **time‑neutral** (does not define or modify time or temporal parameters).

---

## 2. Role of GCO

GCO:

- aggregates outcomes of local coherence evaluations over time and scale,
- detects persistence of low‑cost relational patterns,
- evaluates cross‑scale consistency between subsystems and containers,
- produces structural viability summaries (e.g., coherent / marginal / unstable).

GCO does **not**:

- define a new coherence law,
- perform local update selection,
- directly decide continuation of refinement,
- act as a global controller or scheduler,
- define or require geometry,
- define or modify temporal parameters (γ, ε, pacing).

---

## 3. Module Contract Conditions

- **Derived, not primitive:**
  GCO is derived from LCO behavior; it is not an independent operator.

- **Geometry‑neutral:**
  GCO uses relational structure and LCO outcomes, not geometric constructs.

- **Time‑neutral:**
  GCO does not define, reference, or require global time, and does not alter refinement or temporal weights.

- **Summarizing, not acting:**
  GCO produces structural summaries and viability classifications; downstream modules decide what to do with them.
