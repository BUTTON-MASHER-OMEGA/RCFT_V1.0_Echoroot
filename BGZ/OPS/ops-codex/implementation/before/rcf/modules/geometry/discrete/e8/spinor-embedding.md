## purpose

This document records how the E₈ lattice interacts with the SO(4)-induced update algebra used in the current formalism. It describes the observed behavior of an 8D packing when evaluated under 4D quaternion composition.

---

## dimensional mismatch and restricted update behavior

Sequential relational updates in the current formalism are evaluated using
( SU(2) \times SU(2) ) quaternion composition.

* **600-cell (4D):** Exhibits algebraic closure under this update group in standard-density regimes.
* **Dual 5-demicube (5D):** Exhibits statistical closure via mapping to nearby vertices.
* **E₈ ($4_{21}$) (8D):** Exhibits highly restricted update behavior in current evaluations.

Because E₈ contains a single positive non-identity relational tier, non-identity spinor compositions generally produce targets that do not align with the lattice. In current formulations, no intermediate relational tiers are available to accommodate these updates.

---

## limits of statistical closure

In intermediate-density regimes, the dual 5-demicube maintains coherence by mapping off-lattice updates to nearby vertices.

In E₈:

1. No intermediate or near-lattice vertices are observed due to the single-tier spectrum.
2. Mapping an off-lattice update would require displacements that exceed typical mismatch tolerances in current high-γ evaluations.
3. As a result, non-identity updates are frequently rejected under Local Coherence Operator (LCO) evaluation.

**Outcome:** In current evaluations, admissible updates are predominantly identity-like or highly constrained.

---

## consequences under GCO evaluation

In global continuous evaluations (GCO), this restricted update behavior appears as a regime with very limited relational transitions. The geometry exhibits minimal drift or redistribution and remains relatively stable across recursive updates.

This behavior corresponds to the reduced mismatch accumulation observed in high-γ evaluations.
