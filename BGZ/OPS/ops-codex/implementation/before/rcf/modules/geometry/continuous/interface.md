# Continuous Geometry Interface

## Document Contract

- owner: RCF continuous geometry module
- type: Contract
- scope: projected continuous-shadow records
- authority: tier-7 continuous-shadow representation interface
- upstream dependencies: `/modules/geometry/projection/interface.md`
- status: active
- purpose: expose continuous relational descriptors inherited through projection
- anti-backflow: continuous descriptors may not modify projection or discrete survivors and may not silently acquire metric, dynamical, or physical meaning

## Inputs

The module may consume projection outputs, preserved-relation metadata, smoothed adjacency, and declared approximation profiles.

## Outputs

It may expose continuous-shadow structure, neighborhoods, non-metric descriptors, embedding records, and traceability to projection inputs.

## Prohibitions

The module does not own distance, metric tensors, curvature, flow, forces, fields, or ontology.
