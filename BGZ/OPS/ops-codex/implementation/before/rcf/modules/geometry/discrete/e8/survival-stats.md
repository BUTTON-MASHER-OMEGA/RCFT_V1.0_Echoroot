## **survival profile (high‑γ regime in current evaluations)**

### **1. high combinatorial packing**
The E₈ ($4_{21}$) configuration corresponds to the densest known sphere packing in 8 dimensions, projecting locally as a 240‑vertex adjacency network.

In current evaluations:

- this high packing density is associated with **lower observed mismatch** at elevated γ
- both the 600‑cell and the dual 5‑demicube show **increasing mismatch** in this regime
- E₈ exhibits **comparatively stable** mismatch accumulation under the present update algebra

These observations apply only to the tested candidate set.

---

### **2. single‑tier spectrum and drag behavior**
E₈ exhibits a **single positive non‑identity relational tier (0.5)** in the present formulation.

Observed consequences:

- geometries with multiple tiers accumulate mismatch through tier‑to‑tier mapping
- E₈’s lack of intermediate tiers limits admissible transitions
- this is associated with a **relatively flat mismatch‑vs‑γ profile** in current runs

This behavior is recorded as an empirical property of the current mismatch function.

---

### **3. crossover region (γ ≈ 3.8–4.2 in current runs)**
As γ increases:

- the dual 5‑demicube’s mismatch approaches the recursive‑closure threshold
- its mismatch curve intersects with that of E₈ in the range
  \[
  \gamma \approx 3.8–4.2

Beyond this region, E₈ exhibits the **lowest observed mismatch** among the evaluated geometries.

This is a dataset‑bounded observation, not a universal transition point.

### **4. high‑rigidity regime**
In the high‑γ regime of the present formalism:

- E₈ shows **restricted admissible update pathways**
- the absence of intermediate tiers limits sequential LCO transitions
- this corresponds to a **high‑rigidity behavior** at the upper end of the tested density ladder

This regime reflects the behavior of the current update algebra and mismatch rule.
