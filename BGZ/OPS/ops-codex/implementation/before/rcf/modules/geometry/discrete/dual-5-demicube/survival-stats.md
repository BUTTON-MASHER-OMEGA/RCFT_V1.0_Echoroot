## purpose

This document records the survival behavior of the dual 5-demicube under the RCF survival sieve. It describes its observed persistence in high constraint-density regimes (high γ) in current evaluations, where it appears as a transitional topology between standard-density configurations and high-rigidity limits.

---

## survival profile (supermassive regime)

### 1. hyper-compressed packing

The dual 5-demicube condenses 16 5-cells into a compact 26-vertex configuration. In current evaluations, this high local density is associated with sustained recursive viability under elevated constraint pressure, where less compressed geometries (such as the 600-cell) show reduced persistence.

---

### 2. drag resilience via slush

Unlike the 600-cell, the dual 5-demicube lacks clean irrational (φ) relational tiers. Its inner-product spectrum is anisotropic and irregular (slush-dominated).

While this results in a higher baseline static quantization error \((\mathcal{E}*{static})\), its compressed structure is associated with slower growth in dynamic relational drag \((\mathcal{R}*{drag})\) under increasing γ in current evaluations.

It therefore exhibits a trade-off between low-γ efficiency and higher-γ persistence.

---

### 3. the lower transition boundary \((\gamma \approx 2.5)\)

In current mismatch-function evaluations, as γ increases, the 600-cell’s mismatch values rise relative to the dual 5-demicube. Their corresponding \(F(G, \gamma)\) curves intersect near this threshold.

This intersection corresponds to the observed onset of the transition regime, where the 600-cell is no longer the dominant survivor under the survival sieve.

---

### 4. the upper transition boundary ((\gamma \approx 4.0))

As γ continues to increase, the dual 5-demicube’s mismatch values also rise. At higher γ, its \(F(G, \gamma)\) curve intersects with that of E₈ ($4_{21}$), which shows lower observed drag contribution in this regime.

Beyond this point, the dual 5-demicube no longer appears as the dominant survivor in current evaluations.
