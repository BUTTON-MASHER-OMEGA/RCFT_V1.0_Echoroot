## purpose
This document describes the **plateau‑based representational metric**, a downstream construction that reflects how γ‑plateau structure appears in the continuous shadow.
The plateau metric introduces **no new primitives** and remains fully determined by:

- γ‑plateau assignments inherited from upstream sector structure,
- survivor‑consistent ordering,
- mismatch‑related variation,
- and Π’s smoothing behavior.

The plateau metric is **representational only** and does not influence upstream modules.

## definition
Let \(\Phi\) denote the continuous shadow produced by Π, and let \(\gamma(x)\) denote the γ‑plateau assignment at a point \(x\).
The plateau metric is described as a representational assignment:

\[
d_{\text{plateau}}(x, y)
\]

where:

- \(x\) and \(y\) are points in the continuous shadow,
- the value reflects **plateau‑related relational organization**,
- and no coordinate, manifold, or physical interpretation is assumed.

This assignment:

- is Π‑determined,
- references plateau structure and survivor‑consistent ordering,
- and remains representational and downstream‑only.

## structural properties

### 1. Π‑determinism
The plateau metric is determined by:

- γ‑plateau assignments,
- survivor‑consistent ordering,
- mismatch‑related variation,
- and Π’s smoothing behavior.

No external structure contributes.

### 2. locality
The metric reflects local plateau organization:

- points within the same plateau are associated with reduced representational separation,
- points across distinct plateaus are associated with increased representational separation,
- and no nonlocal operator is assumed.

### 3. relational behavior
The metric describes:

- how plateau organization appears in the shadow,
- how γ‑consistent regions relate representationally,
- and how these observations are described under Π-consistent smoothing.

All behavior is relational and descriptive.

### 4. coordinate‑free and metric‑optional
The plateau metric is described without:

- coordinate charts,
- basis choices,
- affine connections,
- or geometric interpretation.

Any coordinate representation is downstream and optional.

### 5. representational neutrality
The metric:

- does not imply curvature,
- does not imply dynamics,
- does not imply physical distance,
- and does not impose structure on upstream modules.

It is a descriptive representation of plateau‑related relational organization.
