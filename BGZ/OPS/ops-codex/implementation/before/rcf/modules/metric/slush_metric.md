## structural properties

The slush metric is determined by:

- slush distribution,
- mismatch gradients,
- survivor‑consistent structure,
- and Π’s smoothing behavior.

No external structure contributes.

### 2. locality
The metric reflects local slush variation:

- higher slush density is associated with reduced representational separation,
- lower slush density is associated with increased representational separation,
- and no nonlocal operator is assumed.

### 3. relational behavior
The metric describes:

- how slush‑related variation appears in the shadow,
- how local mismatch gradients shape representational proximity,
- how these observations are described under Π-consistent smoothing.

All behavior is relational and descriptive.

### 4. coordinate‑free and metric‑optional
The slush metric is described without:

- coordinate charts,
- basis choices,
- affine connections,

Any coordinate representation is downstream and optional.

### 5. representational neutrality
The metric:

- does not imply curvature,
- does not imply dynamics,
- does not imply physical distance,
- and does not impose structure on upstream modules.

It is a descriptive representation of slush‑related relational variation.
