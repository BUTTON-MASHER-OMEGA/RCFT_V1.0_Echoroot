## purpose
This document describes the **representational outputs** produced by the metric module.
These outputs are **downstream‑only** and provide optional representational structures derived from adjacency‑based, slush‑based, plateau‑based, and composite metrics.
No new primitives are introduced.

All outputs are:

- Π‑determined,
- representational,
- coordinate‑optional,
- and non‑interpretive.

---

## output categories

### 1. representational distance assignments
Each metric construction (adjacency, slush, plateau, effective) produces a representational assignment:

\[
d(x, y)
\]

where:

- \(x\) and \(y\) are points in the continuous shadow,
- the value reflects relational structure described by the chosen metric,
- and no geometric or physical interpretation is assumed.

These assignments are descriptive and downstream‑only.

---

### 2. optional coordinate charts
Downstream modules may define coordinate charts to represent metric structure.
These charts:

- are optional,
- do not influence upstream modules,
- and do not imply manifold structure.

Coordinate choices are representational conveniences only.

### 3. optional metric tensors
A metric tensor may be introduced downstream to encode representational distance assignments in coordinate form.
Such tensors:

- are representational,

They are descriptive encodings of relational structure.
