## purpose

This document records how residual instability in the discrete layer—
mismatch, constraint friction, and local coherence loss—appears in the
continuous shadow after projection.

These residuals are collectively referred to as *slush*.

Slush is treated as a structural remainder of discrete mismatch and
recursion behavior that is not fully removed by Π’s smoothing.
No physical or geometric interpretation is assigned.

## overview

In current evaluations, slush is described as:

- the projection of local mismatch patterns that persist under recursion,

  discrete irregularities,

  constraint pressure changes,

all traces of constraint-associated irregularity.
