# Relational Coherence Framework (RCF)

## Document Contract

- owner: RCF project
- type: Status
- scope: framework identity, architectural posture, and navigation
- authority: project entrypoint subordinate to the RCF invariant kernel
- upstream dependencies: `rcp-1.2.md` and `core/S∞.RCF.Invariants.v1.1.md`
- status: active
- purpose: state what RCF is, how its authority is partitioned, and what it does not claim
- anti-backflow: this overview may summarize the framework but may not expand the invariant kernel or promote optional modules and interpretations into RCF requirements

RCF is a **constraint-based framework** for analyzing systems that persist under relational pressure.

It is not a single theory of physics, cognition, or meaning.
It is a **lens**: a way of filtering structures by survivability, coherence, and constraint.

RCF does not begin by asking *what exists*.
It begins by asking *what survives*.

---

## What RCF Does

RCF provides a **pre-theoretical scaffold** that can be applied across domains:

* physics
* cognition
* social systems
* machine learning
* self-referential frameworks (including RCF itself)

It does this by enforcing a small set of **irreducible invariants** and rejecting anything that violates them.

RCF is:

* eliminative, not additive
* constraint-first, not dynamics-first
* relational, not object-centric
* spectral, not configurational
* modular, not monolithic

If a structure persists under increasing relational constraint, RCF allows it.
If it collapses, RCF lets it go.

No rescue clauses. No patching.

---

## How RCF Is Used

RCF is not “applied” in the traditional sense.
It is **used as a filter**.

A practitioner typically:

1. Takes an existing model, theory, or intuition
2. Strips it down to its relational core
3. Applies RCF invariants as constraints
4. Observes what survives
5. Discards what does not

What remains may resemble known theories — or not.
RCF is indifferent to familiarity.

---

## What RCF Refuses to Do

RCF intentionally refuses several common moves found in other frameworks:

* It does not postulate fundamental entities
* It does not introduce forces to explain failures
* It does not privilege observers
* It does not protect intuitively “nice” outcomes
* It does not preserve legacy structures for comfort

RCF has no interest in being complete.
It has an interest in being **stable**.

---

## Structure of the Framework

At a high level, RCF consists of:

* **Core invariants** (non-negotiable)
* **Modules / lenses** (optional, eliminable)
* **Operational tooling** (out of scope, but compatible)

Only the invariants define RCF.

Everything else is provisional.

---

## Self-Applicability

RCF is required to survive its own constraints.

Its documents, concepts, and interpretations are treated as shards subject to:

* elimination
* refinement
* collapse
* revision

If RCF ever requires special exemption from its own rules, it fails.

---

## A Note on Tone and Intent

RCF is written in a deliberately **non-mythic, minimally interpretive voice** at the core level. Its authority is explicit in document contracts rather than implied by rhetoric.

This is intentional.

Interpretive layers may adopt narrative, metaphor, or intuition —
but the framework itself remains neutral, sparse, and hostile to dogma.

RCF does not ask for belief.
It asks for endurance.

---

## What RCF Is *Not*

The following clarifications are explicit, not defensive.
They exist to prevent category errors.

RCF is **not**:

* ❌ a religion
* ❌ a belief system
* ❌ a worldview that must be adopted wholesale
* ❌ a metaphysical doctrine
* ❌ a theory of everything
* ❌ a replacement for existing physics
* ❌ a cognitive or psychological diagnosis
* ❌ a moral framework
* ❌ an ideology
* ❌ a personality type
* ❌ sacred geometry, numerology, or mysticism
* ❌ a claim of special insight or authority

RCF does not confer status, meaning, purpose, or identity.

If a reader finds themselves *wanting* RCF to do those things,
that impulse itself is something RCF would filter.

---

## Closing

RCF is not defined by what it explains.

It is defined by what it refuses to allow.
