# SMGv2.1 Core Contract

## Purpose

This document is the authoritative scope contract for SMGv2.1.

SMGv2.1 is a downstream domain model and translation bridge.
It consumes upstream structural records and maps them into a Standard Model-facing semantic system.

SMGv2.1 is allowed to be forceful.
Its force applies inside the SMGv2.1 domain model and to downstream models that choose to adopt it.

---

## 1. Imported Facts

SMGv2.1 imports only read-only structural records exposed by upstream interfaces.

### From `gcm_interface`

- Chromatic-12 indexing
- three gamma-plateau groupings
- 36-mode composite structure
- sectorization records
- non-metric adjacency descriptors

### From `physics_interface`

- discrete support records
- continuous support records
- temporal boundary records
- metric boundary records
- amplitude boundary records
- final conversion boundary records

These imported records remain upstream-owned.
SMGv2.1 may bind them.
SMGv2.1 may not redefine them.

---

## 2. SMGv2.1-Local Claims

SMGv2.1 asserts, within its own domain model, that:

- Dual Closure is the central organizing thesis of the Standard Model of Geometry.
- the 36-mode structure is the model-local bridge between upstream survivor structure and downstream Standard Model semantics.
- the Chromatic-12 partition is mapped by SMGv2.1 to the downstream `8 + 3 + 1` semantic structure.
- the three gamma plateaus are mapped by SMGv2.1 to the downstream three-generation semantic structure.
- continuous gauge, spinor, metric, mass, and resonance language is downstream translation language, not upstream ontology.

These are SMGv2.1 claims.
They are not automatically RCF canon.

---

## 3. Binding Voice

Binding files must contain only:

- purpose
- upstream source
- imported structure
- local binding name
- owner and directionality clause
- narrow structural constraints

Binding files must not contain:

- physical commentary
- enforcement declarations
- downstream invalidation language
- metaphysical phrasing
- model-local thesis claims

---

## 4. Translation Voice

Translation files may map upstream structural patterns into downstream semantic forms.

Translation files may be theory-forward.
They must remain scoped with phrases such as:

- SMGv2.1 maps
- within SMGv2.1
- SMGv2.1 treats this as
- downstream semantic form
- SMGv2.1-authorized approximation

Translation files may not present SMGv2.1 mappings as direct upstream facts.

---

## 5. Downstream Obligations

For downstream models adopting SMGv2.1:

- SMGv2.1 dictionary mappings are authoritative within the SMGv2.1 domain model.
- SMGv2.1 policy files define admissible and inadmissible downstream usage.
- failures must be described as invalid under SMGv2.1, not invalid universally.
- exported structures must be treated as SMGv2.1-authorized downstream approximations.

Downstream adoption is opt-in.
Adoption carries the model-local premises.

---

## 6. Anti-Backflow Clause

SMGv2.1 may not:

- modify RCF invariants
- reinterpret upstream geometry ownership
- rewrite `gcm_interface`
- rewrite `physics_interface`
- convert SMGv2.1-local claims into upstream facts
- force downstream semantic labels into upstream records

Upstream records constrain SMGv2.1.
SMGv2.1 does not constrain upstream records.

---

## 7. Non-Goals

SMGv2.1 does not:

- generate new upstream geometry
- define new RCF invariants
- define a universal physics ontology
- prove that its semantic labels are fundamental objects
- require non-adopting downstream models to obey SMGv2.1 policy

---

## RCP-1.1 Compatibility

- imported facts are separated from SMGv2.1-local claims
- downstream obligations are scoped to adopters
- anti-backflow is explicit
- strong thesis language is preserved but ownership-scoped
