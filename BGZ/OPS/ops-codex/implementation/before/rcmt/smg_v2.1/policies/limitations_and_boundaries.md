# SMGv2.1 Limitations and Boundaries Policy

## Purpose

This file defines downstream policy for models adopting SMGv2.1.

These policies are scoped to SMGv2.1.
They are not universal physics decrees.

---

## 1. PDE Breakage Policy

For downstream models using SMGv2.1:

- a continuous singularity is treated as a failure of the SMGv2.1 downstream approximation
- infinite-density or infinite-energy outputs are outside the SMGv2.1 admissible envelope
- singularity-as-object language is invalid under SMGv2.1

SMGv2.1 does not state that all singularity language is universally invalid outside this model.

---

## 2. Renormalization Policy

For downstream models using SMGv2.1:

- renormalization may be used as a downstream computational shortcut
- renormalization may not be treated as an upstream repair
- infinite self-energy loops are invalid under SMGv2.1 when treated as fundamental objects

The SMGv2.1 policy is that boundary records constrain the approximation.

---

## 3. Generation Policy

For downstream models using SMGv2.1:

- the three-generation map is fixed by the SMGv2.1 dictionary
- a fourth generation is outside the SMGv2.1 admissible envelope
- any fourth-generation model requires an explicit SMGv2.1 contract revision

This is a model-local policy.

---

## 4. Final Boundary Policy

Downstream models adopting SMGv2.1 must respect:

- `B_T` as the temporal boundary binding
- `B_g` as the metric boundary binding
- `B_alpha` as the amplitude boundary binding
- `B_final` as the final boundary tuple

If a downstream model contradicts these boundaries, the result is invalid under SMGv2.1.

---

## 5. Non-Adopter Clause

Models that do not adopt SMGv2.1 do not inherit this policy.
SMGv2.1 makes no universal claim over non-adopting models.

---

## RCP-1.1 Compatibility

- policy scope is explicit
- no upstream ownership is rewritten
- invalidation language is SMGv2.1-scoped
