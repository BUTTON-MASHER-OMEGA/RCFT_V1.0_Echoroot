## Current Interpretation

Within the current RCMT interpretation, a meson-like structure is modeled as a bounded containment regime.

Its candidate observable behavior is associated with local closure, bounded internal modes, and transitional internal organization.

The meson-like structure is treated as the containment geometry.

---

## Containment

Meson-like behavior is associated with:

- bounded containment,
- local closure,
- internal behavioral modes,
- and localized organization.

Containment is favored over unrestricted dispersion.

---

## Behavioral Modes

Within this interpretation, quark-like behavior is treated as internal behavioral modes of the containment structure.

These modes are not interpreted as independent survivor geometries.

Their observable relationships emerge through the internal organization of the container.

## Constraint Propagation

Gluon-like behavior is interpreted as internal constraint propagation.

Its candidate functional role is to correlate with bounded containment and internal organization during the retained interval.

This interpretation does not require literal connecting objects.
