## Current Interpretation

Within the current RCMT interpretation, meson-like structures are modeled as transitional containment regimes.

Their limited persistence is associated with bounded internal organization and provisional local closure behavior.

The emphasis is placed on containment rather than constituent ontology.

---

## Closure Topology

Meson-like structures are interpreted as two-mode containment organizations.

These modes are treated as internal behavioral relationships rather than as independent survivor geometries.

Temporary bounded containment is associated with local closure records.

---

## Internal Organization

Within this interpretation, meson-like structures are characterized by:

- bounded containment,
- internal behavioral modes,
- local closure,
- internal constraint propagation,
- and localized organization.

Their observable behavior emerges from the interaction of these internal relationships.
