## Purpose

This contract establishes the architectural interpretation of hadron-like structures within the current RCMT framework.

It defines the local assumptions used by the Hadron Module while preserving compatibility with upstream RCF invariants.

This document is interpretive and downstream-scoped.

---

## Core Principles

### Single Survivor

Hadron-like structures are not modeled as collections of independent survivor geometries.

Within the current RCMT interpretation, a hadron-like structure is treated as a containment regime of a single projectible survivor structure.

---

### Containment

The hadron is treated as the container.

Stable hadronic behavior emerges through closure and bounded internal organization.

Containment is favored over dispersion.

---

### Behavioral Modes

Quark-like behavior is interpreted as internal behavioral modes of the containment structure.

These modes are not treated as independent survivor geometries.

Their observed behavior emerges through local organization and allowed internal relationships.

---

### Constraint Propagation

Gluon-like behavior is interpreted as internal constraint propagation.

Its functional role is to support stable containment and bounded internal organization.

This interpretation does not require literal connecting objects.

---

### Closure

Stable hadron-like structures are interpreted as closure topologies.

Different classes of hadrons correspond to different stable containment organizations.

The emphasis is placed on closure behavior rather than constituent ontology.

---

### Standard Model Mapping

This module provides an interpretive mapping to familiar Standard Model structures.

It is not intended to replace established physics.

The purpose is to provide a relational lens for examining strongly interacting systems.
