## 2. Core Interpretation

Within the current RCMT direction, lepton-like regimes are distinguished primarily by the amount of sustained refinement work required to preserve survivor persistence.

The current interpretation treats mass-like behavior as:

> sustained LCO refinement work required to maintain an admissible survivor configuration under local compression conditions.

This interpretation remains RCMT-local.

---

## 3. Work and Persistence

The current interpretation compares survivor persistence across ordered local evaluation records rather than treating one record as a complete history.

Within this interpretation:

* low-work regimes require minimal refinement effort,
* high-work regimes require greater refinement effort,
* and persistence remains bounded by local admissibility constraints.

No perfect equilibrium state is assumed.

Persistence is maintained within an admissible stability band.

---

## 4. Compression-Regime Structure

The current lepton interpretation organizes charged-lepton regimes as increasing work profiles.

### Electron-Like

Characteristics:

* low refinement burden,
* low mismatch production,
* broad persistence window,
* strong projection stability.

### Muon-Like

Characteristics:

* increased refinement burden,
* elevated compression pressure,
* narrower persistence window,
* reduced persistence duration.

### Tau-Like

Characteristics:

* high refinement burden,
* strong compression pressure,
* narrow persistence window,
* proximity to the current 600-cell persistence boundary.

The current interpretation treats these regimes as persistence states of the same survivor architecture.

---

## 5. Neutrino-Like Regimes

The current interpretation treats neutrino-like regimes as low-work survivor configurations.

Characteristics include:

* low mismatch burden,
* weak observable boundary interaction,
* and broad persistence windows.

The detailed relationship between neutrino-like and charged-lepton work profiles remains unresolved.

---

## 6. Mismatch Interpretation

Within the current RCMT direction, sustained refinement work is associated with mismatch management.

The current interpretation treats increasing mismatch burden as contributing to:

* increased refinement cost,
* reduced persistence stability,
* and elevated decay tendency.

Mismatch is not treated as a physical field within this file.

This file only records the current interpretation of its relationship to survivor persistence.

---

## 7. Decay Interpretation

Within the current interpretation, decay-like behavior is assigned when imported records place the refinement burden outside the survivor's declared admissible persistence window.

Decay is interpreted as:

> renegotiation toward a lower-work admissible survivor configuration.

This interpretation remains exploratory.

---

## 8. Projection Stability

The current interpretation treats projection stability as inversely related to refinement burden.

Lower-work regimes generally exhibit:

* greater projection stability,
* broader persistence windows,
* and lower mismatch production.

Higher-work regimes generally exhibit:

* reduced projection stability,
* narrower persistence windows,
* and increased mismatch management requirements.

This remains a structural interpretation.
