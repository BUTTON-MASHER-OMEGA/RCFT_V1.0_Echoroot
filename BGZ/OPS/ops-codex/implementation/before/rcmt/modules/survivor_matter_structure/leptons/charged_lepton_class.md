## 5. Compression-Regime Structure

The current interpretation treats generation-like behavior as compression/work-regime differentiation.

### Electron-like Regime

* low refinement burden,
* high persistence stability,
* minimal externally resolvable mismatch,
* and strong projection compactness.

### Muon-like Regime

* increased refinement burden,
* elevated compression pressure,
* and reduced persistence window relative to electron-like regimes.

The current interpretation associates this regime with increasing pressure from higher-friction geometric structures.

### Tau-like Regime

* high refinement burden,
* narrow persistence window,
* and instability near the current `4_21` / E8 transition envelope.

Within the current interpretation, tau-like records lose persistence before the model classifies a higher-compression successor as stabilized.
