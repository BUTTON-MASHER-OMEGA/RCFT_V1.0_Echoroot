## Purpose

This document defines the current RCMT interpretation of baryon-like structures.

It establishes the general characteristics shared by stable baryon-like containment regimes while remaining independent of specific Standard Model mappings.

This document is interpretive and downstream-scoped.

---

## Current Interpretation

Within the current RCMT interpretation, baryon-like structures are modeled as stable containment regimes.

Their persistence is associated with bounded internal organization and stable closure behavior.

The emphasis is placed on containment rather than constituent ontology.

---

## Closure Topology

Baryon-like structures are interpreted as three-mode containment organizations.

These modes are treated as internal behavioral relationships rather than as independent survivor geometries.

Stable closure supports persistent containment.

---

## Internal Organization

Within this interpretation, baryon-like structures are characterized by:

- stable containment,
- bounded internal modes,
- persistent closure,
- internal constraint propagation,
- and localized organization.

Their observable behavior emerges from the interaction of these internal relationships.

---

## Constraint Stability

Constraint propagation acts to preserve stable containment.

Local organization favors bounded structures over unrestricted dispersion.

This document does not require literal connecting objects.
