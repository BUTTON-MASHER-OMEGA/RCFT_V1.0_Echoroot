# High-Density Event Classification

## Document Contract

- owner: RCMT Geometry-Behavior

- scope: candidate classification of concentrated local behavioral records
- depends_on: `geometry_behavior_contract.md`, `echoon_field.md`, `mismatch_gradients.md`, and `closure_reinjection_cycles.md`
- status: provisional
- anti-backflow: this file does not define upstream density regimes, geometry candidates, admissibility, or cosmological dynamics

## Purpose

This file defines candidate RCMT classifications for local neighborhoods containing declared concentrations of mismatch samples, echoon density labels, survivor records, or reinjection events.

High density is always relative to an explicit imported density band or a declared module-local threshold. It is not a physical energy density.

## Admission Rule

A `high-density-event-record` must contain:

- the finite local neighborhood;
- source identifiers for every contributing record;
- explicit adjacency support;
- the imported density bands or declared thresholds used;
- the classification rule;
- and provisional status.

The event cannot be inferred from descriptive intensity language alone.

## Candidate Preconditions

A consuming model may classify a neighborhood using:

- persistent mismatch-gradient records;
- overlapping local gradient records;
- high echoon density-band labels;
- multiple locally ordered reinjection events;
- or multiple adjacent survivor-response candidates.

These are classification inputs, not universal causes.

## Collapse-Window Label

A **collapse-window** label may be applied when an imported survivor record is followed by a locally ordered record in which that survivor is changed, unresolved, or absent, and the declared high-density criterion is met.

The label means local reclassification. It does not mean physical destruction or gravitational collapse.

## Reinjection-Storm Label

A **reinjection-storm** label may be applied to multiple imported reinjection-event labels in one connected neighborhood under a declared concentration threshold.

The label does not imply amplification, radiation, or energy release unless a downstream interface independently defines such a correspondence.

## Temporary Geometry-Motif Association

A high-density event may reference an upstream candidate-geometry label, including a 16-cell, 24-cell, 120-cell, or 600-cell label, only when that label exists in the source record.

Geometry-Behavior may report co-occurrence or ordered association. It must not:

- generate a candidate geometry;
- alter its admissibility status;
- call a non-survivor a survivor;
- or infer projection mechanics.

## Survivor Reclassification

Before-and-after survivor records may be described using:

- retained;
- changed;
- unresolved;
- or absent.

Reformation, recombination, and recovery are optional interpretation labels for these record relations, not canonical processes.

## Local Concentration and Clumping

Repeated high-density event records may be grouped when their neighborhoods are connected by explicit adjacency and ordering. The resulting pattern may be called clumping-like.

This module does not infer:

- a global attractor;
- gravitational force;
- large-scale geometry;
- or cosmological evolution.

Those interpretations require downstream contracts.

## Prohibitions

This file does not permit:

- undeclared density thresholds;
- physical collision or energy claims;
- automatic geometric survivorship;
- nonlocal event aggregation;
- metric volume or distance;
- global cosmological conclusions;
- or causal conclusions from correlation alone.

The output is a traceable `high-density-event-record` conforming to `geometry_behavior_contract.md`.
