# Survivor Interaction Patterns

- owner: RCMT Geometry-Behavior

- depends_on: `geometry_behavior_contract.md`, `../survivor_matter_structure/README.md`, `mismatch_gradients.md`, and `echoon_field.md`

## Purpose

This file defines candidate RCMT descriptions for local record changes involving two or more survivor records. **Interaction** means a traceable local association under declared rules; it does not mean a fundamental force or particle exchange.

## Admission Rule

An `interaction-pattern-record` must include:

- identifiers for every survivor record;
- an explicit connected adjacency neighborhood;
- relevant mismatch-gradient, echoon-pattern, or closure-reinjection records;
- independently admissible survivor transitions;
- the classification rule;
- and provisional status.

Co-occurrence alone is insufficient to establish interaction.

## Candidate Channels

### Mismatch Redistribution

A sequence in which adjacent local mismatch samples change while source records remain traceable. The description does not assert transfer of substance or energy.

### Echoon-Associated Coupling

Two or more survivor-response candidates associated with the same valid echoon-pattern record. The description does not establish mediation or force.

### Gradient Alignment

An admissible survivor-record change associated with a local mismatch-gradient ordering. Terms such as approach, separation, and attraction are noncanonical unless supplied by a later metric interface.

### Phase-Cycle Correlation

A declared relation among imported phase-cycle labels in adjacent survivor records. Synchronization may be reported only from explicit ordered records.

### Offset Adjustment

An admissible change between imported offset-regime labels associated with local behavioral records. Charge-like language is a downstream analogy, not a property established here.

## Composite Configurations

A multi-survivor configuration may be classified as retained when:

- its survivor identities remain valid under Matter Structure;
- its adjacency relations remain explicit;
- and its local transition records remain admissible.

The module may compare declared mismatch summaries before and after a local sequence. It may not claim global minimization or a bound state without a separate downstream contract.

## Work-Regime Comparisons

High-work and low-work are comparative labels derived from imported LCO work or mismatch records under one declared profile. These labels are not intrinsic mass classes.

Associations with baryon-like or electron-like survivor labels remain candidate RCMT correspondences inherited from Matter Structure. Geometry-Behavior does not strengthen their status.

## Range

This module has no independent interaction-range primitive. Structural influence extends only through an explicit finite chain of adjacent records. Any continuous or metric range is downstream-owned.

## Prohibitions

This file does not permit:

- force or energy-transfer claims;
- nonadjacent influence;
- implicit global optimization;
- hidden coupling state;
- creation or modification of survivor classes;
- physical charge, mass, or bound-state ontology;
- or use of a downstream correspondence as upstream evidence.

## Output

The output is a traceable `interaction-pattern-record` conforming to `geometry_behavior_contract.md`.
