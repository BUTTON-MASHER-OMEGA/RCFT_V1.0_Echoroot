# **Propagation Contract (PC‑1.0)**
*(RCMT → RCF‑compatible, RCP‑typed)*

This contract defines the upstream semantics of **propagation** in RCMT.
Propagation is the movement of mismatch, mode structure, or survivor‑derived signatures **across adjacency**, prior to any geometric or field‑like interpretation.

Propagation is **not** motion in space, not waves, not fields, not particles.
It is the **update of relational structure** under the invariants of the substrate.

---

## **1. Purpose**

> **Define how mismatch, modes, and survivor signatures propagate across adjacency in the upstream substrate.**

Propagation is the backbone of all downstream interpretations (field‑like, wave‑like, particle‑like), but is not any of those things upstream.

**Type:** Purpose
**Owner:** RCMT propagation module

---

## **2. Ownership**

The propagation module owns:

- the upstream semantics of adjacency updates
- the rules for mismatch propagation
- the rules for mode‑pattern persistence during propagation
- the interface for propagation outputs

The propagation module does **not** own:

- geometric interpretation
- field‑like interpretation
- wave‑like behavior
- metric structure
- causal mechanism

**Type:** Invariant

---

## **3. Propagation Definition**

**Propagation** is:

> **the update of mismatch, mode, or survivor‑derived structure across adjacency under LCO coherence constraints.**

Propagation:

- is adjacency‑first
- is discrete
- is invariant‑preserving
- is coherence‑scored
- is not geometric
- is not continuous
- is not causal in the physics sense

Propagation is **relational update**, not motion.

**Type:** Invariant (category definition)

---

## **4. Propagation Classes**

Propagation may involve:

- **free mismatch propagation**
  (unbound mismatch traveling adjacency)

- **mode‑bearing propagation**
  (mode structure preserved across adjacency)

- **survivor‑signature propagation**
  (stable survivor patterns influencing adjacency updates)

These are **structural classes**, not ontological categories.

**Type:** Observation

---

## **5. Allowed Inputs**

The propagation module consumes:

- adjacency relations
- mismatch categories
- mode structures
- survivor signatures
- LCO coherence scoring
- basin structure

**Type:** Dependency

---

## **6. Allowed Outputs**

The propagation module exposes:

- updated mismatch distributions
- updated mode‑bearing patterns
- updated survivor‑signature influence
- adjacency‑consistent propagation traces

These outputs:

- **are upstream structures**,
- **may be interpreted by projection as motion, waves, or fields**,
- **but are not geometric or field‑like on their own.**

**Type:** Interface Output

---

## **7. Propagation → Projection Interface**

Projection may interpret propagation outputs as:

- motion‑like behavior
- wave‑like behavior
- field‑like variation
- particle‑like trajectories
- geometric‑lane dynamics

These are **downstream interpretations**, not upstream facts.

**Type:** Downstream Interpretation

---

## **8. Prohibitions**

Propagation must not:

- imply geometry
- imply fields
- imply waves
- imply continuous manifolds
- introduce metric structure
- redefine mismatch categories
- redefine survivor behavior
- widen upstream invariants

**Type:** Prohibition

---

## **9. Scope Binding**

All propagation statements must be scoped:

- “within the present formalism”
- “as upstream propagation behavior”
- “prior to projection”
- “in the propagation module”

This prevents downstream leakage.

**Type:** Contract

This is observational, not canonical.

**Type:** Observation
