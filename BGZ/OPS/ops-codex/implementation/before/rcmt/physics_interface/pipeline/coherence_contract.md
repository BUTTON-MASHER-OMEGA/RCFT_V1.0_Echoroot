# **Coherence Contract (CC‑1.0)**
*(RCMT → RCF‑compatible, RCP‑typed)*

This contract defines how **coherence** is evaluated, maintained, and enforced in RCMT.
Coherence is the upstream scoring mechanism that determines:

- which mismatch patterns persist
- which survivors stabilize
- which modes remain valid
- how propagation behaves
- which geometry candidates survive projection

Coherence is **not** energy, not probability, not amplitude, not a field, and not a metric.
It is the **consistency score** of relational structure under the invariants.

---

## **1. Purpose**

> **Define the upstream coherence scoring rules that govern stability, propagation, survivor formation, and mode persistence.**

Coherence is the backbone of upstream stability.

**Type:** Purpose
**Owner:** RCMT coherence module

## **2. Ownership**

The coherence module owns:

- the definition of coherence scoring
- the evaluation of relational consistency
- the stability criteria for survivors
- the persistence criteria for modes
- the propagation‑compatibility scoring

The coherence module does **not** own:

- geometric interpretation
- field‑like interpretation
- wave‑like behavior
- causal mechanism
- metric structure

**Type:** Invariant

## **3. Coherence Definition**

**Coherence** is:

> **the relational consistency score assigned to mismatch, modes, survivors, and geometry candidates under the invariants of the substrate.**

Coherence:

- is upstream
- is discrete
- is invariant‑preserving
- is adjacency‑first
- is not geometric
- is not probabilistic
- is not energetic

Coherence is **structural consistency**, not physics.

**Type:** Invariant (category definition)

Coherence applies to:

- **mismatch coherence**
  (whether mismatch patterns remain valid)

- **survivor coherence**
  (whether survivors maintain identity)

- **mode coherence**
  (whether modes persist across evaluations)

- **geometry‑candidate coherence**
  (whether tilings remain stable)

These are **evaluation classes**, not ontological categories.

**Type:** Observation
