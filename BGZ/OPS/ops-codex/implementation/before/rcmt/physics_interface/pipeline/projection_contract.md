# **Projection Contract (PCX‑1.0)**
*RCMT → RCF‑compatible, RCP‑typed*

Projection is the **boundary layer** between upstream RCMT structure and downstream physics‑shaped modules.
It is the **only** module allowed to produce geometric‑lane artifacts, field‑like behavior, or continuous‑looking structure.

Projection does **not** generate upstream structure.
Projection **interprets** upstream structure.

This contract defines the full responsibilities, constraints, and interfaces of the projection layer.

---

## **1. Purpose**

> **Define the complete projection layer: its inputs, outputs, responsibilities, prohibitions, and interface with physics modules.**

Projection is the **translator** between RCMT and physics.

**Type:** Purpose
**Owner:** Projection layer

---

## **2. Ownership**

Projection owns:

- the **interpretation** of upstream structure
- the **smoothing** of adjacency‑first updates
- the **mapping** from discrete → continuous‑looking
- the **exposure** of geometric‑lane artifacts
- the **exposure** of effective field‑like behavior
- the **exposure** of spinor‑like and mode‑like structure

Projection does **not** own:

- mismatch categories
- survivor formation
- mode structure
- geometry candidates
- coherence scoring
- propagation rules
- basin structure
- upstream invariants

**Type:** Invariant

---

## **3. Projection Definition**

**Projection** is:

> **the layer that interprets upstream relational structure (adjacency, mismatch, survivors, modes, geometry candidates) into downstream geometric‑lane artifacts under smoothing and consistency constraints.**

Projection:

- is interpretive
- is smoothing
- is downstream
- is not generative
- is not causal
- is not ontological

Projection is **the interpreter**, not the substrate.

**Type:** Invariant (category definition)

---

## **4. Allowed Inputs**

Projection may consume:

- **adjacency**
- **mismatch categories**
- **survivor structures**
- **mode structures**
- **geometry candidates**
- **propagation traces**
- **coherence scores**
- **basin structure**

These inputs are **upstream objects**.

**Type:** Dependency

---

## **5. Allowed Outputs**

Projection may expose:

- **geometric‑lane coordinates**
- **continuous‑looking structure**
- **effective curvature‑like variation**
- **effective field‑like behavior**
- **spinor‑like orientation profiles**
- **mode‑like harmonic structure**
- **particle‑like persistence signatures**
- **region‑like or domain‑like structure**

These outputs:

- **are downstream interpretations**,
- **are not upstream objects**,
- **are not ontological fields or geometry**.

**Type:** Interface Output

---

## **6. Projection Smoothing Rule**

Projection applies:

> **adjacency‑consistent smoothing**
> **coherence‑weighted interpretation**
> **basin‑compatible mapping**
> **mode‑preserving transformation**
> **survivor‑identity preservation**

This smoothing is:

- not metric
- not energetic
- not causal
- not dynamical

It is **interpretive smoothing**, not physical smoothing.

**Type:** Interpretation Rule

---

## **7. Projection Interpretation Rules**

Projection may interpret:

- **free mismatch** → *effective field‑like variation*
- **survivors** → *matter‑like structure*
- **modes** → *spin/charge/harmonic‑like behavior*
- **geometry candidates** → *geometric‑lane structure*
- **basins** → *region‑like or phase‑like domains*
- **coherence** → *stability or mass‑like persistence*

These are **interpretations**, not upstream facts.

**Type:** Downstream Interpretation

---

## **8. Prohibitions**

Projection must not:

- generate new upstream structure
- redefine mismatch categories
- redefine survivors or modes
- introduce metric structure
- introduce continuous manifolds
- introduce fields or forces
- introduce causal dynamics
- widen upstream invariants
- collapse upstream distinctions
- imply ontological geometry

Projection is **strictly interpretive**.

**Type:** Prohibition

---

## **9. Projection → Physics Interface**

Physics modules may assume:

- geometric‑lane coordinates
- effective field‑like behavior
- spinor‑like structure
- curvature‑like variation
- particle‑like persistence
- region‑like domains

Physics modules may **not** assume:

- upstream geometry
- upstream fields
- upstream forces
- upstream metric
- upstream continuity
- upstream causality

Physics modules must treat projection outputs as **effective**, not ontological.

**Type:** Interface Contract

- “as a downstream interpretation”
- “in the projection layer”
- “not an upstream object”

This prevents leakage across the firewall.

**Type:** Contract
